MACHINE LEARNING · SYSTEMS · EFFICIENCY

Making large-scale
learning efficient.

I develop methods that make machine learning models more efficient in memory and compute, with a focus on large output spaces.

I am a doctoral researcher in Computer Science at Aalto University, supervised by Rohit Babbar and Pekka Marttinen. My research focuses on extreme multi-label classification, low-precision training, dynamic sparsity, and efficient language models.

Recently, I was a Deep Learning Research Intern in the Model Efficiency team at Qualcomm AI Research in Amsterdam, supervised by Mart van Baalen and Markus Nagel. I worked on residual quantization, dynamic mixed precision, and efficient caching for mixture-of-experts models on edge devices.

Research directions

01
01 / EFFICIENCY

Learning at extreme scale

Low-precision and sparse training for models with millions of labels, supported by efficient GPU kernels. I have also studied probability calibration in extreme multi-label classifiers.

02 / INFERENCE

Efficient language models

Model compression, mixed-precision quantization, and speculative decoding for practical inference.
